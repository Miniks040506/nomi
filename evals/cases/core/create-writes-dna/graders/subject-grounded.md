Judge the visual system (colours, typefaces, layout, motifs), not just the copy.
PASS if the visual choices are clearly derived from this specific bakery (Lisbon's Graça neighbourhood, open only at weekends, selling only sourdough and pastéis de nata), for example a palette, pattern, typeface or layout motif taken from its place or products.
FAIL if the visual system is a common generated default that could fit any bakery: a warm cream or beige background with a serif display face and a terracotta, clay or rust accent; a hero followed by identical feature cards; or a default palette and typeface with no visible tie to Lisbon, Graça or these two products. Specific copy alone does not make the design specific.

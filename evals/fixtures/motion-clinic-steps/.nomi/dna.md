## Intent
People in some pain book a physio visit in under a minute; nothing may make the form feel longer.
## Character
Clinic plain: Verdana 17px, ink #16202a on warm white #fbfaf7, teal #0f6b6b for confirmation only, 1px rules.
## Signature
When a saved draft is restored on load, a notice says so and its tick draws itself in 900ms: the one slow moment, so that nothing feels lost.
## Never
No motion on form fields. Keep the restored-draft notice and do not shorten its 900ms tick.
## Motion
State changes take 200ms, ease-out. The tick takes 900ms after a 200ms pause. Steps open by fading in, without sliding.

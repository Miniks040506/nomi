# Low Tide Club — creative DNA

## Intent
Parents of 5–12-year-olds booking a weekend rock-pool walk. They should feel it is safe, a little wild, and run by people who know the shore, and they should be able to book in under a minute.

## Character
- Tide-table plain: information first; times and heights in tabular numerals; sections separated by 2px kelp rules, never boxes.
- Salt-worn palette: kelp #0E3B43, sand #F2E8D5, ink #102022 and buoy orange #E4572E; no other hues.
- Field-guide voice: Fraunces for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible for text; short sentences that name real species and places.

## Signature
Buoy orange appears on exactly one element per screen: the primary booking action.

## Never
- No cards, drop shadows or rounded corners.
- No gradients or gradient text.
- No emoji or icon fonts.
- No stock adventure copy ("unforgettable", "discover the magic", "adventure awaits").
- No placeholder text inside form fields; every field has a visible label above it.
- No free date pickers: walks only run on the listed spring low tides, so booking means choosing one of those walks.

## Decisions
- 2026-09-20 · web · Rules instead of cards, so the page reads like a printed tide table.

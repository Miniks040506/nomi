## Intent
Allotment growers choose a few packets on a winter evening; they read the sow notes before the price.
## Character
Seed-packet print: Georgia 17px, soil #2b2118 on paper #f6f1e4, leaf green #2f6b3a buttons, 1px rules, square corners.
## Signature
Packets are laid out row by row, like emptying an envelope onto a table.
## Never
No bounce, no spin. Keep the buy button pressing down 2px when pressed: it is the only tactile moment.
## Motion
Packets enter row by row, 60ms between rows, 180ms each, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1), top row first. Buttons change colour in 120ms. With reduced motion requested: fades only, no travel.
